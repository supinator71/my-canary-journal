# Archive Triage

Decision made 2026-07-24: **unlist the irreducibly political essays**, keep the
portable ones as raw material for re-editing, and open the new direction with a
"why this is changing" post.

Unlisting on Substack hides a post from the archive and feed while keeping the
URL live — so anyone holding a direct link still reaches it, and search engines
may keep it cached for a while. Delete is the stronger action where the concern
is exposure rather than tidiness (see the patient-material notes below).
